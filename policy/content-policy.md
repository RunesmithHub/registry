# Content policy

Names, summaries, icons, banners, screenshots, READMEs and release notes on Runesmith Hub must:

- **Not impersonate** Runesmith, Runesmith Hub, their badges or logos, or another plugin or publisher.
- **Be accurate.** Screenshots show the plugin; the summary describes what it does.
- **Be respectful.** Nothing hateful, harassing, sexual, violent or illegal.
- **Not advertise** unrelated products or services.

Administrators decide borderline cases and explain their decision in the registration or tracking issue.
