# Publisher policy

Every plugin on Runesmith Hub follows these rules. Breaking them can get a version rejected, a plugin removed or a publisher suspended.

1. **No malware.** No code that harms users, their machines, their data or their accounts, hidden or not.
2. **Be honest about what your plugin does.** Declare every capability your plugin and its libraries use, with a reason a user can
   understand. Your name, summary, icon and README describe the plugin truthfully.
3. **Ask before collecting data.** Do not send anything about the user, their projects or their machine anywhere unless the README says
   so and the plugin asks the user first.
4. **No mining, advertising or tracking** inside Runesmith.
5. **No obfuscation.** The published source must be readable and must be what the package is built from.
6. **Keep your account safe.** Use two-factor authentication on GitHub, protect your release tags, and tell us right away through private
   vulnerability reporting if your account or repository may have been compromised.
7. **Respond to security reports** about your plugin within 14 days.
8. **Respect licenses.** Your plugin is under an OSI-approved license, and so is everything it bundles in a compatible way.
